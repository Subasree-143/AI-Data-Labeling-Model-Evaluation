# Visual Quality Evaluation

## 1. Overview

Visual quality evaluation examines the technical and
aesthetic characteristics of AI-generated images.

It focuses on how well an image is constructed,
presented, and visually maintained.

## 2. Key Evaluation Components

### Image Clarity
- Sharpness and resolution.
- Visibility of important details.
- Unnecessary blurring or distortion.

### Composition
- Balance between visual elements.
- Appropriate spacing and positioning.
- Subject visibility and framing.
- Foreground and background organization.

### Lighting and Shadows
- Consistency of light sources.
- Natural-looking shadows.
- Appropriate illumination.
- Reflection and shading consistency.

### Proportions and Structure
- Subject proportions.
- Object dimensions.
- Anatomical consistency.
- Structural alignment.

### Color and Texture
- Color balance.
- Texture consistency.
- Natural transitions.
- Unusual patterns or visual irregularities.

## 3. AI-Generated Artifacts

Common visual artifacts include:

- Distorted facial features.
- Unnatural hand structures.
- Extra or missing fingers.
- Warped objects.
- Repeated visual elements.
- Inconsistent textures.
- Incorrect object intersections.
- Unnatural reflections and shadows.

## 4. Visual Quality vs. Artistic Style

Artistic style refers to the intended visual appearance
of an image.

Visual defects refer to unintended inconsistencies
that reduce image quality.

For example:

- Watercolor textures are stylistic characteristics.
- Distorted object boundaries may indicate artifacts.
- Exaggerated proportions may be intentional in cartoon art.
- Inconsistent anatomy may indicate a generation defect.

## 5. Evaluation Method

1. Inspect the overall image.
2. Examine important subjects and objects.
3. Review complex visual regions.
4. Identify inconsistencies and artifacts.
5. Assess their impact on the overall image.
6. Compare the visual quality of both outputs.

## 6. Expected Outcome

Visual quality evaluation helps identify technical
weaknesses and distinguish intentional artistic
choices from unintended generation defects.
