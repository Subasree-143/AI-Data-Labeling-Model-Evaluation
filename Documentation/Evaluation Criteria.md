# AI Evaluation Criteria

## Overview

AI-generated images are evaluated using five major dimensions
to assess their quality, relevance, and consistency.

Each dimension addresses a different aspect of the
generated output.

## 1. Overall Preference

Measures the overall effectiveness of an image in satisfying
the user's request.

Evaluation considerations:
- Alignment with the original prompt.
- Overall visual presentation.
- Quality and completeness.
- Balance between requested elements.
- Comparative strengths of both outputs.

## 2. Instruction Following

Measures how accurately the generated image follows
the user's instructions.

Evaluation considerations:
- Requested objects and subjects.
- Specified actions and interactions.
- Background and environmental requirements.
- Requested artistic style or reference.
- Composition and positioning requirements.

## 3. Visual Quality

Measures the visual presentation and technical quality
of the generated image.

Evaluation considerations:
- Image clarity and sharpness.
- Lighting and shadows.
- Object proportions.
- Composition and spatial consistency.
- Color balance and visual details.
- Anatomical and structural consistency.

## 4. AI Artifacts

Identifies visual defects that may result from
AI image generation.

Evaluation considerations:
- Distorted hands, faces, or body parts.
- Unnatural object connections.
- Repeated or duplicated elements.
- Warped shapes and inconsistent textures.
- Unrealistic reflections and shadows.
- Inconsistent physical structures.

Note:
Artistic interpretation should not automatically be
classified as an AI artifact.

## 5. Correctness

Measures the accuracy of visible and verifiable details.

Evaluation considerations:
- Spelling and text accuracy.
- Correct representation of requested objects.
- Readable labels and visual information.
- Accurate numbers or symbols.
- Consistency of visible details with the prompt.

## Distinguishing Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Main Question |
|-----------|---------------|
| Overall Preference | Which output is better overall? |
| Instruction Following | Did it follow the prompt? |
| Visual Quality | Does it look visually well-executed? |
| AI Artifacts | Are there generation-related defects? |
| Correctness | Are the visible details accurate? |

## Evaluation Principle

Each dimension should be assessed independently.

For example, an image may follow the requested artistic
style while still containing distorted objects or
visual inconsistencies.

A strong evaluation identifies these differences rather
than treating every weakness as the same issue.
